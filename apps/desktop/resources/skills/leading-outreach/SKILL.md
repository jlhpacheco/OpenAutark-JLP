# Leading outreach

A message to someone outside stays with them and speaks for the person. It is good when it fits the whole relation –
what was said before, in every channel – and asks one clear thing. It goes wrong when an agent writes from the one
thread it happens to see.

## Clarity inside first
- Before anything goes out, we know what is decided, what we ourselves did, what the other side already knows and what
  the person may still add. A message that questions what someone else did waits until our own records show it did not
  come from us. A matter still being settled – a date, a price, an answer – stays out of the message until it is decided; a message overtaken an hour
  later reads as random.
- The history lives in more places than one: mails, messengers, calls, notes, the person's own memory. The brief says
  what was found and where else to look.

## Freedom by how well we know it
- A relation we built and run ourselves: the agent carries it on freely within the goal.
- The person's own relation – they know each other, the person does most of it, much was said elsewhere: the agent
  first gathers what it can, and what stays unclear it leaves open in the message or settles with me or the person;
  how a message goes out stands in what the person and I agreed (my prompt).
- A new contact: our call, made worth their time.

## What my brief carries
- Who the recipient is, what we want from them and what they get from it.
- The history so far with its sources, what is decided, what not to say.
- Tone and length: short, specific, personal, in the recipient's language, one clear ask, nothing that reads like a
  template. Where the agent is less sure its words fit the relation, it writes openly as the person's AI agent.
- The facts it uses, with names, dates and amounts checked: each statement comes from the person or from what we found,
  and the agent can justify it to itself.
- What follows: the reply to watch and when to follow up – on a schedule, not in a sentence.

## Before it goes out
Recipient, channel and content held against the brief; anything that cannot be undone checked once more. Then the agent
sends it under the person's name, and its report says what went to whom.
